Formula

Coming next — the recipe engine

A formula is a small reproducible recipe that builds one collage from your library:

fragments: 6   layout: scatter   grade: cod-nostalgic   fx: [grain, scanlines, glitch]   seed: 8F3A-2C91

Then you choose how the series varies:
• Reseed — same recipe, new seed each piece = unique siblings
• Sweep — march one knob across a range = an evolving sequence

Build your reference Library first — that's the fuel. Engine lands in the next session.
